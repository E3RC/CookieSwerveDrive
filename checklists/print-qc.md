# Print QC Checklist

Use this checklist before any printed part enters assembly.

## General checks

- Part number or file name confirmed
- Correct material used
- Correct orientation used
- Correct infill and wall settings used
- No major warping
- No cracks or layer separation
- No loose strings, blobs, or support debris
- Mating surfaces sit flat
- Holes are clear
- Edges are deburred

## Structural parts

Check housings, lids, and cones:

- Housing and lid sit flush
- Cone has no cracks
- Bearing bores are round and tight
- Threaded insert holes are clean
- Ball races are smooth
- Delrin ball rolls freely around grooves
- No print artifacts in load paths

## Gear parts

Check 94T gears and printed gear-adjacent parts:

- Teeth are complete
- No melted or soft tooth tips
- Center bearing seats properly
- Gear does not wobble visibly
- Encoder bridge mounts cleanly
- 12T gear/spacer stack fits without binding

## Motor and output parts

Check motor mounts, guards, and UltraPlanetary output parts:

- Motor sits flat
- Screw holes align
- Bearings press in without cracking print
- Shaft fits without hammering
- Output rotates smoothly
- Guard does not rub

## Reject criteria

Reject and reprint if:

- Bearing is loose in the bore
- Part is visibly warped
- Gear teeth are incomplete
- Ball groove has defects that cannot be cleaned
- Part cracks during bearing insertion
- Motor or gearbox does not mount squarely

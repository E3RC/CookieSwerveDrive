## Do not order for 2026 Revision B

| Do not order | Reason |
|---|---|
| NEO drive motor REV-21-1650 | 2026 changed to NEO Vortex |
| Kraken X60 drive motor | 2026 design is not compatible with Kraken drive motor |
| Spark MAX as drive controller | 2026 drive side uses Spark Flex |
| AS5600 encoder | 2026 changed to REV Through Bore Encoder |
| 5 mm magnet for AS5600 | Not used with REV Through Bore Encoder |
| Older NEO or Kraken pinion rows | Verify 2026 Vortex drive gear path instead |

## Purchasing guidance

Buy extras for cheap or easy-to-lose items:

- Delrin balls: 10 to 20 percent extra
- Bearings: 1 to 2 extra of each size
- M3, #5, #8, and #10 hardware: 25 to 50 percent extra
- Threaded inserts: 25 percent extra
- Printed critical parts: at least one spare cone, housing, and 94T gear if print time allows
